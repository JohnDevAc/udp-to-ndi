NDI
NDI is a registered trademark of Vizrt NDI AB.
The official NDI Runtime redistributable is included in Setup and used only
when no supported NDI runtime is already installed. It displays its own
license agreement. The NDI runtime remains separately installed and is not
removed when UDP to NDI is uninstalled.
https://ndi.video/
https://docs.ndi.video/all/developing-with-ndi/sdk/software-distribution

Saved slots are held in the current user's LocalAppData/UdpToNdi folder.
Uninstalling UDP to NDI preserves these settings and all Windows NDI settings.
